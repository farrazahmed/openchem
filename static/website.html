About Us
Built on Trust,
Driven by Markets
Montage Commodities is an international commodity trading house headquartered in the UAE. We specialise in the physical trading of fertilizers, steel, and sulphur — connecting major producers across the Middle East, Central Asia, and beyond with consumers worldwide.

Global Footprint

Active trading corridors spanning the Middle East, South Asia, East Asia, South America, and Africa.

Risk-Managed

Performance-Driven

Capital-efficient execution with hurdle-rate discipline and annualized return benchmarking across all desks.

What We Trade
Core Commodity Verticals
Three focused desks with deep domain expertise in their respective markets, enabling superior price discovery and execution.

Fertilizers

We trade granular and prilled urea from the world's leading producers in the Middle East and Central Asia, supplying agricultural markets across South Asia, Southeast Asia, Brazil, and East Africa.

Steel

Our steel desk trades HRC, rebar, and billets — sourcing from major mills in Turkey, CIS, and East Asia, and delivering to construction and manufacturing markets across the Middle East, South Asia, and Africa.

Sulphur

Trading both granular and lump sulphur from major oil-and-gas-derived supply sources in the Gulf. Sulphur is a critical input for phosphate fertilizer production and industrial chemical processes.
